# E-Commerce Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis

A customer analytics and segmentation study based on the Brazilian E-Commerce public dataset (Olist).

## Highlights

- **RFM Modeling**: Calculates Recency, Frequency, and Monetary scores to evaluate customer lifetime value.
- **Unsupervised Learning**: Applies clustering algorithms (K-Means) to group customers into behavioral segments.
- **Multi-Table Relational Join**: Integrates relational datasets including orders, items, reviews, customers, geolocation, and product categories.
